# 14 — User Onboarding & Personal Data Integration (Phase 20 Preparation)

**Status:** Infrastructure Complete & Ready for User Credentials  

---

## Next Steps When You Are Ready:

1. **Add Your Resumes:**  
   Place your 10–15 master resumes (PDF or DOCX) into `C:\sukesh\job\data\resumes\`.
2. **Configure Your Profile:**  
   Edit your verified facts in `C:\sukesh\job\config\profile.json` (template provided).
3. **Log In to Portals (Session Persistence):**  
   Run `scripts/login_helper.ps1` to open the dedicated browser, log into LinkedIn/Naukri/Indeed with your MFA, and preserve the authorized session.
4. **Set Your Target Roles & Preferences:**  
   Define job titles, preferred locations, and salary thresholds in `C:\sukesh\job\config\preferences.json`.
5. **Review Applications in Dashboard:**  
   Open `http://localhost:8000` to review matched jobs and approve applications with a single click.
